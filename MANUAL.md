## 2. Care

You start with an **egg**. It hatches after 3 minutes, or after you tap it 3 times.

### The four stats
| Stat | Goes down when | Refill with |
|---|---|---|
| 🍖 Fullness | a little every 20 min | 🍎 Feed, Catch the fruit |
| ⚡ Energy | a little every 20 min, mini-games | 🌙 Sleep |
| 😊 Joy | hungry or dirty (below 30) | 🎮 mini-games, petting, favorite berry |
| 🫧 Hygiene | poop | 🫧 Bath, Clean up game |

### Feed 🍎
- **Red / Blue / Green berry**: Fullness +25
- Every Pokémon has one **favorite berry**. It gives Fullness +35 plus Joy and Bond, and from then on it shows on the profile.
- **Candy**: a little Fullness but lots of Joy. Watch the weight!

### Sleep 🌙
- Energy refills quickly while asleep, and **it wakes up on its own at 100 Energy.**
- At night (20:00–06:00) your Pokémon gets sleepy.

### Bath 🫧
- Hygiene goes to 100 and poop is cleaned up.
- A full Pokémon poops now and then (up to 3). Poop lowers Hygiene.

### Petting 👆
- Click your Pokémon to raise Joy and Bond a little.

### ⚠️ Care mistakes and running away
- If any stat drops to **10 or less**, you get a **care mistake** (at most one per hour).
- Each care mistake delays evolution and lowers Bond.
- If **all four stats stay at 0 for an hour**, your Pokémon runs away. 😢

## 3. Mini-games

Press 🎮 to pick one of four mini-games. Playing costs a little Energy and Fullness.

| Game | Control | Ends when | Rewards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bounce the ball** | Move your Pokémon with the mouse | 3 misses | Joy, speed training, weight loss |
| **Catch the fruit** | Move your Pokémon with the mouse | 10 seconds | Fullness, speed training |
| **Rhythm memory** | Repeat the order of the glowing buttons | first mistake | Bond |
| **Clean up** | Click the dirt spots | 3 spots pile up | Hygiene, Bond |

## 4. Growth, evolution and farewell

- Your Pokémon gains **1 level per hour of running time**. Weight slowly drops over time.
- At its **evolution level**, with **all stats at 40 or more**, the `✨ Evolve ✨` / `💤 Later` buttons appear.
  - Each care mistake pushes the evolution level back by 1.
  - Pokémon that evolve by other means (stones, trade…) can evolve at Lv.36.
  - If there are several branches, one is picked at random.
- After the **farewell age** in running time (setting, 3 days by default), `⭐ Say goodbye ⭐` appears.
  - A new egg arrives after the farewell. The better you cared for your Pokémon, the higher the chance the next egg is **shiny**.
- You can also let it go at any time with **🍃 Release** in the right-click menu.

> Pokémon can hatch shiny, with gender differences or as regional forms. Choose which generations hatch in Settings.

## 5. Profile and medals

Right-click → **📋 Open profile**. Flip through 6 pages.

1. **Gyms**: your badges. Click a badge to `Challenge`
2. **Info**: name, gender, form, care streak, bond, favorite berry
3. **Battle stats**: Attack, Defense, Speed, and `⚔️ Start a wild battle`
4. **Moves**: the 4 moves it can use
5. **Medals**: see below
6. **Growth**: next level, evolution condition, care mistakes, weight

| Medal | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Reached Lv.10 / 25 / 50 | Reach that level |
| Favorite found | Find its favorite berry |
| 7-day care | Care for it 7 days in a row |
| Max bond | Bond 100 |
| Final form | Reach the last evolution stage |
| Perfect body | Lv.5+, weight 0, no care mistakes |

## 6. Battles and catching

Start a wild battle with `⚔️ Start a wild battle` on the profile, or **wait for one to appear** while idle.

### Actions
| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fight** | Choose one of your 4 moves |
| **Dodge** | 70% to dodge, but if it fails the next hit does double damage |
| **Bag** | Use items such as Potions |
| **Run** | Leave the battle |

- **Type matchups** and **STAB** (×1.5 for moves of its own type) apply.
- **Status conditions**: burn, poison, bad poison, paralysis, sleep, freeze
- **Stat moves**: moves with no power (Growl, Swords Dance…) raise or lower Attack, Defense, Speed and more, up to ±6 stages.
- **Turn order**: the faster side usually moves first, but the slower side has a 20% chance to go first. Paralysis halves Speed.

### Catching
- After you win, a `Catch` button appears. Lower HP means better odds (10% → 35% at half HP or less → 70% at 20% or less).
- If your party is full, you can choose who to send away.

### Results
- **Win**: attack training, more Bond, an offer to learn a new move, sometimes items and Mega Stone shards
- **Lose**: Joy and Energy go down

> 💡 A wild Pokémon that shows up while you're away **runs automatically after 1 minute without input**.
> Wild Pokémon are within ±2 of your level and come only from the generations chosen in Settings. Legendary and shiny ones are very rare.

## 7. Gyms

- On profile **page 1 (Gyms)**, click a badge and press `Challenge`.
- Challenge **64 leaders**, Kanto to Paldea, **in order**.
- **First win**: badge + 2 Hyper Potions + 3 Poké Balls (+ 3 Mega Stone shards once you have the Mega Ring)
- **Rematch**: fight any leader you've beaten again, for smaller rewards.

## 8. Mega Evolution

### What you need
1. **Mega Ring**: earned with **8 gym badges**.
2. **Mega Stone**: made from **10 shards**.
3. The Pokémon's **Mega sprite** (get it from the updater)

### Where shards come from
| Source | Chance |
|---|---|
| Wild battle win | 10% |
| Gym win / rematch | always |
| Items found on walks | 30% of finds |

Shards are **most likely to be your lead Pokémon's stone**, then your **party's stones**. See your collection in the bag's **💎 Mega** tab.

### In battle
- Press **💎 Mega Evolve** to Mega Evolve **without using a turn**, once per battle.
- If there are several forms (Charizard X/Y…), you can choose.
- The Mega form's stats and types last until the battle ends.

### On walks
- Pokémon that can Mega Evolve **sometimes Mega Evolve for 3 minutes** while walking, then change back (random form).
- The first time is 1–3 minutes after the walk starts, then every 10–20 minutes. Each Pokémon changes on its own timing.

## 9. Party and bag

### Party 📂
- Up to 6 Pokémon. Right-click → **📂 View party**
- `Make lead`: raise this Pokémon on the main screen.
- `Swap`: switch the order of two members.

### Bag 🎒
- **🎒 Bag** tab: Potions, Poké Balls and more. Usable in battle too.
- **💎 Mega** tab: Mega Ring status and stone/shard counts

## 10. Walk mode

Start with `Ctrl` + `W` or **Walk mode** in the tray menu. The widget hides and **your party roams the desktop.**

- Drag a Pokémon with the mouse to move it.
- Now and then they **rummage through the grass** for items (up to 5 per walk). You get a summary when they come back.
- Time still passes, but stats **never drop below 30**. Safe to leave it running!
- The whole party grows at **⅓ of the normal speed**.
- **To end**: press `Ctrl` + `W` on a Pokémon, or double-click the tray icon / **Show TamaPoke**
- You can't start a walk during a battle, mini-game or evolution.

**Desktop Pokémon (1–6)**, **Pokémon size (0.5–5×)** and **Stay on top of the taskbar** apply right away, even mid-walk. Pokémon that are too big are shrunk to fit the screen.

## 11. Settings and updates

### Settings (`Ctrl` + `O`)
| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Language | 한국어 / English (Pokémon and move names too) |
| Show tray notifications | Balloon tips when hungry, etc. |
| Farewell (days) | How many days until you can say goodbye |
| Desktop Pokémon | How many walk (1–6) |
| Pokémon size | Walk size (0.5–5×) |
| Stay on top of the taskbar | Keep walkers on the taskbar |
| Hatchable generations | Regions that eggs and wild Pokémon come from |
| Factory reset | Erase everything and start over |

### Update TamaPoke (`Ctrl` + `U`)
1. Pokémon data (names, base stats)
2. Pokémon move sets (level-up moves)
3. Check and download missing sprites
4. Check for and install the latest TamaPoke

> On first run, if sprites or data are missing, the updater opens with the needed items already checked.

## 12. FAQ

**Q. My Pokémon is invisible or the screen is empty.**
A. The sprites aren't downloaded yet. Press `Ctrl` + `U` → **Check and download missing sprites**.

**Q. Where is my save?**
A. `save.json` in the program folder. The previous save is kept as `save.bak.json`, and a damaged save is restored from it automatically. If it can't be restored, the damaged file is kept as `save.corrupt-<date>.json`. Settings live in `settings.json`.

**Q. How do I move to a new version?**
A. Unzip the new version and copy `save.json` and `settings.json` from the old folder. Or use option 4 in the updater to install it automatically.

**Q. Does time stop when my PC is off?**
A. Yes. Time only passes **while TamaPoke is running**: levels, the farewell age and stat decay all count running time. If you leave it running while you're away for a long time, put it in **walk mode** so stats stay at 30 or above.

**Q. I don't see the Mega Evolve button.**
A. You need the Mega Ring (8 badges), that Pokémon's Mega Stone and its Mega sprite. It can be used once per battle.

**Q. It won't evolve.**
A. Check the evolution condition on the profile's **Growth** page. All stats must be 40 or more, and care mistakes push the level back.
